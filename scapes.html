Meet the Garlic Scape

Garlic scapes are the tender flower stalks that emerge from hardneck garlic plants in late spring and early summer. We harvest them while they are young, before they become woody.

They have a fresh, green garlic flavor that's milder than a mature clove. Slice them into a skillet, blend them into pesto, or use them anywhere you'd like a gentle garlic kick.

SeasonTypically June on the North Fork
StorageRefrigerate in a loose bag; use while fresh
PreparationRinse and trim any tough ends

Cooking with Scapes

Five easy ways to cook with fresh garlic scapes, from weeknight pasta to a golden, cheesy quiche.

RECIPE 01 · 10 minutes · 1 cup

Garlic Scape Pesto

A bright, fresh sauce for pasta, sandwiches, or roasted vegetables. No nuts needed.

Ingredients

  • 8–10 garlic scapes, chopped
  • 1 cup packed fresh basil
  • ⅓ cup grated Parmesan
  • ½ cup olive oil, plus more as needed
  • 1–2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Method

  1. Pulse the scapes, basil, and Parmesan in a food processor until finely chopped.
  2. With the motor running, drizzle in the olive oil until spoonable.
  3. Add lemon juice, season to taste, and toss with hot pasta or spread on toasted bread.
RECIPE 02 · 10 minutes + chilling · ½ cup

Garlic Scape Butter

A simple way to bring early-summer garlic flavor to bread, corn, and potatoes.

Ingredients

  • ½ cup softened butter
  • 2–3 garlic scapes, very finely minced
  • 1 teaspoon lemon zest (optional)
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Mix softened butter with minced scapes, lemon zest, and salt.
  2. Spoon onto parchment, roll into a log, and chill until firm.
  3. Slice over hot vegetables, toast, or grilled chicken.
RECIPE 03 · 15 minutes · 2–3 servings

Sautéed Garlic Scapes

The easiest way to enjoy scapes: tender, lightly charred, and a little sweet.

Ingredients

  • 1 bunch garlic scapes
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Lemon wedge (optional)

Method

  1. Trim the tough ends and cut the scapes into 2-inch pieces.
  2. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat; add scapes and cook for 5–8 minutes, stirring, until lightly browned and crisp-tender.
  3. Season with salt and pepper and finish with lemon if you like.
RECIPE 04 · 25 minutes · 2–3 servings

Garlic Scape Pasta

An easy summer pasta that puts fresh scapes front and center.

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces spaghetti or linguine
  • 6–8 garlic scapes, thinly sliced
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • ¼ teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)
  • ¼ cup grated Parmesan
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Cook pasta in salted water; reserve ½ cup pasta water before draining.
  2. Warm olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add sliced scapes and pepper flakes; cook 4–6 minutes until tender.
  3. Add pasta, a splash of reserved water, Parmesan, and lemon juice. Toss until glossy, adding more pasta water as needed.
  4. Season and serve immediately.
RECIPE 05 · 20 minutes prep + 40–50 minutes baking · 6–8 servings

Garlic Scape & Cheddar Quiche

A golden, savory quiche with tender garlic scapes and sharp cheddar. Perfect for brunch, lunch, or a light supper.

Ingredients

  • 1 unbaked 9-inch pie crust
  • 1 cup garlic scapes, finely chopped (about 6–8 scapes)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil or butter
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup shredded sharp cheddar cheese
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • Pinch of nutmeg (optional)

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F. Fit the crust into a 9-inch pie dish. Prick the bottom with a fork, line with parchment and pie weights, and blind-bake for 12 minutes. Remove weights and bake 5 minutes more.
  2. While the crust bakes, heat the oil or butter in a skillet over medium heat. Sauté the chopped scapes for 4–5 minutes, until softened. Let cool slightly.
  3. Whisk together eggs, cream, salt, pepper, and nutmeg. Spread scapes and cheddar evenly in the crust, then pour the egg mixture over them.
  4. Bake for 35–45 minutes, until the edges are set and the center has just a slight wobble. If the crust browns too quickly, shield the edges with foil.
  5. Rest 10–15 minutes before slicing. Serve warm or at room temperature.

Kitchen tip: Cook the scapes before adding them to the filling for a sweeter, more tender bite. This is a suggested recipe to accompany our farm photo.
